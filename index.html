Sweet Tooth cell

Scientific Name:

Glucovora

Discovered by / at:

Atai, under a microscope in his CCLab class, Fall 2026

Family:

Glucovora belongs to a family of tiny single cells that drift in water and feed only on sugar. It is the shyest and hungriest member of its family.

Habitat:

Small drops of sugary water.

Appearance:

A small, pale oval cell with a striped edge, a soft teal inside and a few pink and magenta spots.

Key Characteristics:

  • Never stops wobbling.
  • Runs from the mouse.
  • Eats only sugar.
  • Grows with every bite.

What we know so far about Sweet Tooth cell? The Sweet Tooth cell was first spotted as a tiny, jittery speck that never sat still. Under a stronger lens, scientists saw that it was hunting: whenever it touched a grain of sugar, the sugar vanished and the cell grew 20% bigger. Its hunger feeds itself, because the bigger it grows, the farther it can reach, so every grain it eats makes the next one easier to catch. Luckily, it is extremely timid. It senses anything coming close and speeds away, and the closer the danger gets, the faster it flees. Once the danger is gone, it slowly calms down and goes back to wobbling. Researchers who managed to touch one found that it freezes and turns dark, and it has never been seen moving again.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Move your mouse close to the Sweet Tooth cell and it will run away, faster the closer you get. Leave it alone to watch it eat the white sugar dots and grow. If you are quick enough to click right on it, you catch it and it freezes forever. Reload the page to meet a new one.
